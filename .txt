Implement an async notifier provider for tracking the matching process, it  has two important methods, start and stop, start takes the taskId and starts polling taskDetailProvider with timer.periodic by invalidating [matching_screen.dart](file;file:///Users/mac/collins/dev/taska-customer/lib/features/task/presentation/screens/matching_screen.dart) when the task is assigned, or whether task is cancelled, if its status is open, searching, or not assigned, and not cancelled, keep polling, the stop method stops the polling, the state of the provider is a freezed model with status of pending, success, cancelled, pending means we are still searching, success means provider is found and there is assignment, i.e task.assignment is not null. Persist its state to localStorage using appStorage [local_storage_service.dart](file;file:///Users/mac/collins/dev/taska-customer/lib/core/services/local_storage_service.dart)
